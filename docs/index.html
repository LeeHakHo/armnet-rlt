Armnet Remote Platform

Train and evaluate robot learning methods on real SO-101 arms over the internet.
Your GPU runs the policy and the learner; a remote cell runs the episodes, resets the scene and scores each one.

How the system runs

How to use

  1. Install
    uv sync --locked
scripts/setup_local.sh learner
  2. Start your policy server and learner on your GPU
    rlt-local policy-server --checkpoint <your checkpoint>
rlt-local online --algorithm <your model>* --run-id my-run

    * Baselines: rlt, dsrl, and iterative DiT via scripts/run_iterative_dit.sh

  3. Send an actor to a cell
    rlt-submit-actor --task push_green_button --learner-key <config>:my-run --num-rollouts 100
  4. Evaluate on ID and OOD scenes
    rlt-submit-eval --frozen-rlt-checkpoint <frozen actor> --num-rollouts 10

Every flag is in the README.

Evaluation protocol

ID rail in training range rail-low below range rail-high above range bright15 light 30 → 15 bright5 light 30 → 5 tilt1550 top camera tilted tilt1750 tilted further

Each condition: 3 seeds × 5 rollouts, no exploration noise, 30 s limit. A safety trip counts as a failure.
The seed fixes the rail positions, so every policy sees the same scenes.

Baselines

Baseline results

push_green_button on one SO-101 cell. Each baseline trains for up to 6 hours, then runs 105 evaluation rollouts.

Iterative DiT step 1,800 · 2.1 h35 / 105 (33%)ID 10/15 · OOD 25/90
RLT step 20k · 5.9 h40 / 105 (38%)ID 10/15 · OOD 30/90
DSRL step 390k · 5.6 h63 / 105 (60%)ID 9/15 · OOD 54/90

105 evaluation rollouts per method: 15 in-distribution (ID) + 90 out-of-distribution (OOD, 6 conditions × 15).
No safety trips in any evaluation. DSRL's lead comes from the OOD conditions.

Successes out of 15. Each method uses its latest evaluated checkpoint within 6 hours of training.
